Google Search Console setup

1. Add the Domain property: itnedumangad.online
2. Verify ownership using the DNS TXT record Google provides.
3. Submit: https://itnedumangad.online/sitemap.xml
4. Inspect the homepage and important public pages and use Request Indexing.

Do not put a guessed verification token into the site. If Google gives you an HTML meta tag, add that exact tag to the <head> of index.html.
